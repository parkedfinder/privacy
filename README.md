# Parked Privacy Policy

*Effective October 14, 2026*

Parked saves your parking pin, an optional photo, and an optional note **only on your device**, including the Parked Home Screen widget.

## What we use
- **Location (While Using):** to drop a pin where you parked and give you directions back.
- **Camera / Photos:** only if you choose to attach a photo of your spot.

## What we don't do
We don't collect, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking across apps or websites. When you tap Navigate back, Apple Maps opens with the pin you saved.

## Deleting your data
Tap **Clear spot** in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored.

## Contact
Questions or support: [open an issue](https://github.com/parkedfinder/privacy/issues).
